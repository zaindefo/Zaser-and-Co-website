# Zaser & Co Editorial Rebuild Design

## Objective

Rebuild the complete Zaser & Co website as one chapter-driven editorial system while preserving every public route. The experience must communicate that Zaser diagnoses business complexity, offers exactly two productised engagements, and produces concrete analysis, systems, and roadmaps.

## Brand Direction

- Primary ink: `#182040`; deep chapter ink: `#0F1428`; structural navy: `#1D2348`.
- Accent: `#782000`, used sparingly; paper: `#F3EEE5`; supporting white and approved greys.
- Display: Bebas Neue. Authority accent: Instrument Serif. Body: Plus Jakarta Sans. Labels/data: DM Mono.
- Signature gesture: real engagement artefacts become the interface. Scorecards, matrices, process maps, dashboards, and roadmaps replace generic icons and decorative technology motifs.
- Raster imagery uses a limited-ink, screen-print/risograph language. Meaningful text is never baked into generated images.

## Narrative System

The global story is: business tension → Zaser point of view → two engagements → working method → tangible outputs → insight → conversion. Home carries the complete argument. Interior routes use distinct immersive chapters while preserving reading and task completion.

Home moves through the editorial hero, brand thesis, two service decisions, dual-lane method story, artefact field, engagement outputs, insights, and a full-screen closing statement. Service pages translate each engagement into a bespoke process sequence. Industry and local SEO pages connect sector tensions to the same two services. Insights become evidence-led editorial articles without fabricated stories. Audit and contact pages end in one shared, accessible Formspree flow. Policies prioritize long-form readability with a sticky index and complete source-document content. StockPulse is framed only as an example of a system Zaser can build.

## Motion and Interaction

- Editorial reveal: clipped heading and label entrance.
- Chapter motion: sticky or scroll-linked composition on major scenes with native scrolling.
- Micro interaction: small image scale, arrow travel, and color inversion.
- Each route family has its own composition; no universal fade-up or duplicated pinned scene.
- Mobile converts complex scenes to intentional sequential layouts.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables pins, parallax, and large transforms while leaving all content visible.

## Content Integrity

The site sells only AI Audit & Implementation and Management & Operations Strategy. Remove all named clients, testimonials, fabricated case studies, unsupported figures, invented ROI, and placeholder contact data. Replace them with engagement artefacts, methods, deliverables, frameworks, and clearly labeled worked examples. Policies must match the supplied 15-policy Word document. Contact uses `hello@zaserandco.com`; WhatsApp is removed.

## Technical Direction

Keep Next.js 16 App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind, GSAP, Framer Motion, Lenis, Formspree, static generation, existing canonicals, and existing routes. Use typed local content modules rather than a CMS. Centralize the lead payload as `name`, `business`, `email`, `message`, `language`, `inquiryType`, and `sourcePage`. Treat motion as progressive enhancement and raster media as responsive, optimized assets.

## Acceptance

All 21 existing routes build and remain reachable. No unapproved proof claims, stale colors, placeholder phone numbers, or contradictory policies remain. Forms handle validation, submission, success, and failure accessibly. Desktop, tablet, mobile, keyboard, reduced motion, structured data, sitemap, and production build checks pass.
